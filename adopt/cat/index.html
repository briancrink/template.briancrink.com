    Fairness panel: how every animal is shown
    • All animals are on one page. No pages, no featured slots, no paid placement.
    • Every tile is the same size, and no pet is promoted.
    • The default order is a random shuffle, reshuffled each visit. It never uses time in care, age, looks, or photo quality.
    • Search and filters dim non-matches instead of removing them. "Show only matches" is the visitor's choice.
    • Picking any tag moves matching animals to the front. Clear it and the mixed order returns.
    • Newest, youngest, and A to Z sorts are chosen by the visitor, never by us.
    • The numbers below cover the entire catalog and update as animals are added or adopted.
